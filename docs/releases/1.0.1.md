# Velvet 1.0.1

**Watch page**

- In a window with the related videos beside the video, Subscribe is at the right of the channel row. The Like, Watch Later, Save, Download and Share buttons fill the row under it, with their names.
- In full screen and in narrow windows, the buttons stay in a compact row next to the channel.
- All the buttons have the same height. Before, the Share and Subscribed buttons were a little taller or shorter than the others.
